Behind the Lens

I'm Zarif, a Dubai-based videographer, video editor, and digital artist. I create short-form content, brand videos, and cinematic reels — blending technical precision with a strong aesthetic eye.

Alongside my creative work, I'm completing a BSc in Information Technology at Middlesex University Dubai, which gives me an edge in understanding digital platforms, content tools, and fast-turnaround workflows.

I shoot on mobile and edit in CapCut, producing content that punches above its weight. Every frame is intentional. Every cut is deliberate.
